# QueryTrends.ai — AI Discoverability

**Is your business in the AI answer, or is your competitor?**

QueryTrends is an independent AI discoverability company founded by **Maria Myers** (MainEntry LLC). It measures how a business shows up when people ask ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, Claude or Grok a buying question, finds which competitors get recommended instead, and identifies the public signals behind those results: then helps fix them.

## Discovery Scout: website readiness scanner

*From hidden to findable.* A free tool that checks how ready a business website is for search **and** AI discovery.

**How it works**
1. Enter the website address, business name, city/area and main service (or mark that the business has no website).
2. The website checks run automatically.
3. Enter a name and email to see the free **Visibility Score**, shown as percentage rings for:
   - Website SEO health
   - Speed
   - Google readiness
   - Bing readiness
   - AI / LLM readiness

**Evidence first. Always.** Checks that can't be verified are marked *Not checked* and left out of the score, so the number never pretends to know more than it does.

## The discoverability framework

Every assessment looks at six observable dimensions:

| Dimension | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| AI / LLM observations | How AI assistants answer commercially important questions about the market |
| Search & SEO foundations | Crawlability, speed, technical health |
| Entity clarity | Whether machines can tell clearly who the business is and what it does |
| Structured data | Schema markup that lets machines read the business |
| Authority & citation signals | Where the business is mentioned and trusted |
| Competitive clarity | Who gets recommended instead, and why |
